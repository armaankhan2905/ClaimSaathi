Section 4.3 · Specific waiting period · p. 17

Listed illnesses and procedures

“Expenses related to the treatment of the listed conditions, surgeries or treatments shall be excluded until the expiry of 24 months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy with the Company… (iv) Joint replacement surgery…”

What it means

Knee or hip replacement is only covered after you have held this policy for 2 years without a break, counted from your first policy, not the latest renewal.

Waiting periodEnded 12 Mar 2024 for you
Section 4.1 · Room rent · p. 15

Room, boarding and nursing

“Room rent, boarding and nursing expenses are payable up to 1% of the Sum Insured per day. Where the insured opts for a room category above the eligible limit, associated medical expenses shall be payable in proportion…”

What it means

You can claim up to ₹5,000 a day for the room. Pick a costlier room and the insurer may cut other bills too, in the same ratio.

LimitProportionate deduction risk
Section 5.9 · Permanent exclusions · p. 21

Maternity expenses

“Medical treatment expenses traceable to childbirth (including complicated deliveries and caesarean sections incurred during hospitalisation) except ectopic pregnancy…”

What it means

Delivery and pregnancy costs are not covered at all on this plan. Only an ectopic pregnancy is an exception.

ExclusionNot covered
Section 3.2 · Pre and post hospitalisation · p. 9

Medical expenses before and after admission

“Relevant medical expenses incurred during 60 days immediately before the date of admission and 90 days immediately after discharge, provided a claim for in-patient hospitalisation is admissible…”

What it means

Tests, medicines and follow-ups from 60 days before and 90 days after a hospital stay are paid, if the main claim is accepted.

To: Grievance Redressal Officer, Navjeevan General Insurance14 Nov 2025

Subject: Request to review repudiation of claim CLM-24-08817 under policy NGI/HL/2025/448812

Dear Sir/Madam,

My claim of ₹1,80,000 for right total knee replacement at City Care Hospital, Pune (18 Sep 2025) was rejected on 02 Oct 2025, citing the 24-month waiting period under Clause 4.3.

Grounds for review

  1. Clause 4.3 §4.3(iv) counts the waiting period from the inception of the first policy. My first policy began on 12 Mar 2022.
  2. The enclosed receipts show continuous renewal with no break in cover §8.2. On the date of surgery I had 42 months of continuous cover.
  3. The rejection letter treats the policy as effective from 12 Mar 2025, which is the latest renewal date, not the inception date.

I request that you review the decision and settle the claim. If you rely on any other ground, please state it in writing with the clause reference.

Yours faithfully,
Rohit Verma
